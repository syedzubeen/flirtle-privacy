Flirtle: Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 July 2026 · Last updated: 20 July 2026

This policy applies to the Flirtle Android application (package com.zubeen.flirtle). Flirtle helps you write dating-profile prompt answers and first messages. Flirtle is built to keep your dating life private: there is no account and no sign-up, and Flirtle never asks for your name, phone number, or email.

Data that stays on your phone

Your drafts, the profile snippets you paste for the opener assistant, your "About you" details, your generation history and favorites, and all app settings are stored locally on your device (Android DataStore) and are never uploaded by Flirtle.

Data that leaves your phone

When the cloud engine generates a suggestion, the prompt text, your draft (or pasted profile snippet), and your About-you details are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the answer. That request is handled under Google's own terms and privacy policy. If you instead use the on-device model, generation happens entirely on your phone using an offline model and nothing about the request is sent anywhere.

Data we collect and share

Flirtle's own code collects no personal information, has no account or login, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We never receive or store the content of your drafts, prompts, or generated suggestions on any server we control: the only place that content is transmitted to is Google's Gemini API, and only when the cloud engine is used, as described above.

Permissions

  • Internet: used for cloud-engine generation requests, the one-time on-device model download, and to request and display ads.

Third-party services

Google Gemini API: powers the cloud generation engine described above. Governed by Google's Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob: Flirtle shows ads (including rewarded ads to unlock extra daily suggestions) through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use a device advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information. Flirtle does not share your drafts, prompts, or personal data with AdMob for advertising purposes. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA / UK): where required, Flirtle asks for your consent before serving personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your Android system settings.

Data retention and deletion

We store none of your data on any server we control, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Local data (drafts, About-you details, history and favorites, settings) lives only on your device and can be cleared at any time from within Flirtle (History → Clear, or Settings), or by clearing the app's data/uninstalling it from Android system settings.

Children

Flirtle is intended for users old enough to use dating platforms (17+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
